The rule

A term is called when its last 7 days are at least 25% above the three weeks before the prior week, the prior week was already 10% above that base, the rise is still holding, and the demand index is at least 10. The same term is not called again within 28 days. Calls are never edited or deleted. Raw ledger: signals.json.
