Korea commerce calendar

The dates that move Korean beauty and food demand, prices and shipping, and what to do before each one. Retailer sale dates follow each retailer's own announcement; dates marked 'expected' follow the usual pattern and are confirmed about a week before. Reviewed 19 Nov 2026.
