Before you import

United States
  • Facilities that make, pack or store the food must be registered with FDA (renewed every two years), with a US agent for foreign facilities.
  • FDA must receive Prior Notice before each shipment arrives; shipments without it are refused and held at the importer's cost.
  • The US importer must run a Foreign Supplier Verification Program (FSVP) for the Korean supplier.
  • FDA does not pre-approve foods, labels or importers; labels must meet US rules (English, Nutrition Facts, allergens).

Source: FDA: Importing human foods
