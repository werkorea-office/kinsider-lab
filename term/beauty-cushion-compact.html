Before you import

If it carries an SPF claim

Tone-up creams and cushions often carry an SPF. With an SPF claim, the product is an OTC sunscreen drug in the US and must use only FDA-monograph UV filters; without the claim it can be sold as a cosmetic. Confirm which version the supplier is offering.

Source: SGS: FDA adds bemotrizinol to the OTC sunscreen monograph

United States
  • The Korean manufacturing facility must be registered with FDA and each product listed under MoCRA (enforced since 1 July 2024). Registration is renewed every two years.
  • A responsible person (brand, packer or distributor named on the label) lists the product, keeps safety substantiation and reports serious adverse events to FDA within 15 business days.
  • Registration and listing are not FDA approval. Treat any supplier claim of an 'FDA-approved cosmetic' with caution.
  • Ask the supplier for: FDA facility registration number, product listing number, English label, full INCI ingredient list, GMP (ISO 22716) certificate.

Source: FDA: Modernization of Cosmetics Regulation Act (MoCRA)

European Union
  • Every product needs an EU-based Responsible Person (Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009, Article 4). For a Korean brand this is usually the importer or an appointed service provider.
  • The Responsible Person holds the Product Information File (PIF) with the Cosmetic Product Safety Report (CPSR) and notifies the product on the CPNP portal before it is placed on the market. One notification covers the EU/EEA.
  • Labels must meet EU rules (ingredients, warnings, Responsible Person address, local language).
  • Ask the supplier for: formula and INCI list, safety data for the CPSR, stability and challenge tests, GMP (ISO 22716) certificate.

Source: EUR-Lex: Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009

United Kingdom (Great Britain)
  • A UK-based Responsible Person has been required since 1 January 2021; an EU notification does not cover Great Britain.
  • The UK Responsible Person notifies each product on the SCPN portal before sale and keeps the PIF.
  • Ask the supplier for the same file set as for the EU; many service providers handle CPNP and SCPN together.

Source: GOV.UK: cosmetic product notifications (SCPN)
