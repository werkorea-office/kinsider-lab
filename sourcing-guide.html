For new brands

Making your own K-beauty brand: Korean OEM/ODM sourcing guide

Most K-beauty brands do not own factories. They work with Korean contract manufacturers, which is why a new brand can launch a Korean-made product without building a lab. This is how it works. General guidance, not legal advice; confirm every term with the manufacturer. Reviewed 19 Nov 2026.

OEM vs. ODM

OEM: you bring the formula and specification; the factory produces it. ODM: the factory's own lab develops or adapts the formula for you, and you sell it under your brand. Most new brands use ODM.

Korea's largest cosmetic ODMs, such as Cosmax and Kolmar Korea, also make products for many well-known global brands. Many smaller, specialist makers (sun care, masks, hair, colour) accept smaller first orders.

What to expect

Minimum order: set per product and per packaging item; large ODMs generally expect larger runs than specialist makers, and stock formulas usually allow smaller first orders than custom ones. Timeline: development and sampling, stability testing, packaging sourcing and production usually take several months from brief to shipment. Cost drivers: active ingredients, packaging (often the largest variable), and order size.

Ask early who sources packaging and who owns the formula. If you may switch makers later, agree formula ownership in writing.

Documents to ask for

Full ingredient list (INCI) and percentages where your market requires them; certificate of analysis per batch; GMP (ISO 22716 / CGMP) status of the factory; stability and preservative-efficacy test results; safety data for the EU/UK Responsible Person (CPSR); and, for the US, facility registration and product listing under MoCRA. Sunscreens are regulated as drugs in the US and need separate handling. See the import checklist.

Where to find manufacturers

KOTRA's buyKOREA lists Korean suppliers and can arrange introductions. Korean beauty trade shows, such as K-Beauty Expo Korea, gather many contract manufacturers in one place. Before you brief a maker, check which ingredients and formats are rising in Korea now on the daily dashboard; Korean makers already have formulas for what Koreans are buying.
