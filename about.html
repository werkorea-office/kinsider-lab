About K-INSIDER LAB

K-INSIDER LAB measures what Korean consumers are shopping for, every day, and publishes it in English for buyers, distributors, retailers and brand teams outside Korea. Korea is where many beauty and food trends start; our job is to show that demand early, with numbers you can check.

What we publish

  • A daily demand index for K-beauty products, ingredients and brands and for K-food, from Naver shopping-search clicks.
  • Breakout calls when a rise holds for two weeks, with every result checked after 7, 14 and 28 days and kept public.
  • Official Korea Customs export figures by product and destination market.
  • An import checklist for the US, EU and UK, with sources.

Principles

  • Data first: we report measured demand and official figures, never paid placements.
  • No product verdicts: we do not tell you a product is good or bad; we show what Korean shoppers do.
  • Nothing hidden: methods, watchlist and the full breakout ledger are public, misses included.
