From Korea to the world: how K-trends travel

Documented cases of Korean beauty and food trends that reached Western shelves, how they got there, and what the gap between Korea and the rest of the world looked like. The lesson: by the time a trend is viral abroad, it has usually been mainstream in Korea for years. Reviewed 19 Nov 2026.

PDRN (salmon DNA): clinic treatment in Korea, then topical skincare abroad

PDRN started in Korea as an in-clinic regenerative treatment, best known through Rejuran, and was common there both as a topical and an in-office facial long before Western coverage. US consumer media (CNN Underscored, NBC Select) ran explainers in August 2025, and the trend then moved from injectables to everyday serums and creams.

Gap: years. Signal to watch: a clinic ingredient showing up in Korean drugstore products. Sources: Happi, NBC Select, Bloomberg

Frozen gimbap: a Korean startup's product sells out Trader Joe's

Allgot, a gimbap maker founded in 2020 in Gumi, launched frozen gimbap at Trader Joe's in early August 2023. A TikTok taste-test drew millions of views, the first 250-ton shipment sold out within weeks, and stock ran short until late October. Buyers then went looking for more frozen gimbap suppliers and Costco talks followed.

Gap: gimbap is an everyday Korean food; the export opportunity was a frozen, shelf-stable format. Signal to watch: formats that solve shipping (frozen, retort, dried), not just dishes. Sources: NBC News, KED Global, Korea.net

Snail mucin: a Korean staple becomes Amazon's #1 beauty product

COSRX's Advanced Snail 96 Mucin Power Essence went viral on TikTok and reached #1 in Amazon US Beauty & Personal Care in 2022, temporarily selling out. The line had sold 6 million units by May 2023, and the #COSRX hashtag passed 2 billion TikTok views.

Gap: snail mucin had been a Korean skincare ingredient for over a decade before it peaked on Amazon. Signal to watch: an ingredient with steady, boring demand in Korea plus a clear texture or result that films well. Sources: Beauty Packaging, Happi

The pattern, and how to use this site

1) Korean clinics or Korean consumers adopt it. 2) Korean drugstores (Olive Young) carry many versions. 3) Korean exports rise. 4) A creator abroad films it and Western retail sells out. The money is in steps 1 to 3.

Use the daily demand dashboard for step 2, market profiles and export data for step 3, and the commerce calendar to time orders.
