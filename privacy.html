Privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026. This policy explains what personal data K-INSIDER LAB (operated by DecisionS, 3F, 235-1 Gaenggogae-ro, Chungju-si, Chungcheongbuk-do, Republic of Korea; contact brief@kinsider-lab.com) collects and why.

What we collect

  • Email subscription: your email address and, if you give them, company and role. We use them to send the weekly brief, breakout alerts you opted into, and report links. You confirm by email before anything is sent (double opt-in).
  • Visit counts: the site counts visits per day without cookies; your browser stores a local flag so a visit is counted once a day. No IP addresses or identifiers are stored by us.

Service providers

Kit (email delivery and list management), Formspree (form submissions), GitHub Pages (hosting) and Cloudflare (network and visit counter) process data on our behalf, some of them in the United States.

Your choices and rights

  • Every email has an unsubscribe link; unsubscribing stops all emails at once.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time by writing to brief@kinsider-lab.com. Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under the GDPR, or Korea under PIPA) you may also object to processing or complain to your data protection authority.
  • We keep subscriber data while you are subscribed and delete it within 30 days of a deletion request.

We do not sell personal data and do not use it for advertising.
